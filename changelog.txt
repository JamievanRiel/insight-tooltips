Version: 1.0.0
Date: 23. 10. 2026
  Features:
    - Initial release.
    - Mining drills: output including speed and productivity bonuses, and when the resources under the drill run out.
    - Inserters: chest-to-chest throughput, matching the measured swing times of Factorio 2.1.
    - Chests and cargo wagons: items stored, and the fill or empty rate measured while you hover.
    - Electric poles: when the accumulators of the network are full or empty.
    - Crafting machines with productivity: progress towards the next free craft.
    - Remote interface: collect, watch, unwatch and inserter_throughput.
